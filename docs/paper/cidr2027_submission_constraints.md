# CIDR 2027 Submission Constraints (for DriftBench demo paper)

Source:
- https://www.cidrdb.org/cidr2027/cfp.html

Checked on:
- May 8, 2026

## Key constraints to enforce

1. Contribution types
- Papers (max 6 pages).
- Demos (video or description, max 6 pages).

2. Formatting
- Double-column ACM `sigconf` style.
- Submission PDF must not exceed 6 pages including references and appendix.

3. Authorship limit
- Each person can be author/co-author on at most one submission of any kind.

4. Important dates (all 11:59pm Pacific Time)
- Submission: August 4, 2026
- Notification: October 6, 2026
- Camera-ready: December 4, 2026
- Conference: January 24-27, 2027 (Amsterdam)

5. Acceptance/presentation
- In-person attendance by at least one author is mandatory.

## Practical implications for our current paper rewrite

1. Keep the submission narrative product/demo oriented, not only vision.
2. Prioritize system capabilities and walkthrough scenario over extensive taxonomy detail.
3. Use concise architecture + interface descriptions (CLI/MCP/service) with one end-to-end flow.
4. Reserve expanded discussion/appendix material for final camera-ready version.
